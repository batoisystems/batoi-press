# Batoi Press 4.0.4

Social Publishing lets site owners connect one Facebook Page, Instagram
professional account, X account, LinkedIn member account and YouTube channel.
Owners and administrators can send saved public pages and posts from the editor.
Press stores credentials encrypted and shows delivery status for each destination.

Facebook, X and LinkedIn share a link. Instagram requires a public JPEG, and
YouTube requires an MP4 with the uploader’s chosen visibility and audience.
Owners supply access tokens from their provider apps and replace expired tokens
in Admin. See the [Social Publishing setup guide](../social-publishing.md) for
permissions and publishing steps. Automated checks cover simulated provider
responses. Live account verification is pending.

Back up the installation and apply the signed 4.0.4 ZIP through **Admin → Updates**.
PHP 8.3 or later is required. See [update recovery](../update-recovery.md) for
rollback instructions.
